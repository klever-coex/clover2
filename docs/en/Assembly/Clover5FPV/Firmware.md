## Flashing to Betaflight

To configure the quadcopter for flying in FPV mode, replace PX4 with Betaflight according to the {doc}`Firmware/FirmwareBetaflight` guide.

## Switching to PX4

To use the quadcopter for programming, replace Betaflight with PX4 according to the {doc}`Firmware/FirmwarePX4` guide.
