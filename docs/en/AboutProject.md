`clover2` is an open-source development platform designed specifically for flying robotic systems. 
As the successor to the original [clover](https://github.com/copterexpress/clover) project, it features a redesigned architecture and has been fully migrated to the ROS 2 ecosystem.

The project is intended both for those who are just beginning to explore robotics and for experienced developers who need a flexible foundation for research and application.

## Why clover2?

There are few solutions for flying robotics that simultaneously meet both requirements:

- **Ease of Entry**. Platform to start programming and flying immediately without needing a deep background in robotics.
- **Flexibility & Scalability**. A powerful foundation supporting advanced autonomous navigation, computer vision, swarm intelligence, and custom research projects.
  
In the current robotics landscape, developers often face a difficult choice between: 
* Proprietary systems that are easy to use but offer limited customization
* Low-level components that offer total control but require massive configuration and integration.

## Who is it for?

**Beginners.** `clover2` provides pre-configured system images, ready-to-use containers, project templates, and practical examples to guide you from your first flight to your first line of code without complex initial setup.

**Experienced Developers.** With a  modular ROS 2 architecture and fully open-source codebase, you can use `clover2` to develop autonomous navigation algorithms, implement sophisticated computer vision, manage quadcopter swarms, or execute complex academic research.
